Welcome

I am an aerospace engineer and MIT Ph.D. specializing in aircraft design, alternative-fuel safety architectures, and flight vehicle testing. My work bridges multi-physics analytical modeling with hands-on hardware prototyping, manufacturing, and testing.

Based in the Greater Boston area (Medford, MA), I am seeking full-time roles focused on aerospace R&D, systems engineering, and design/test engineering, especially those with a strong hands-on or physical hardware component.

Expertise & Core Competencies

  • Aircraft Design & Systems: Multidisciplinary optimization, alternative-fuel systems (hydrogen/methane), requirements definition, and safety frameworks.
  • Testing & Fabrication: UAV flight testing, wind-tunnel experimentation, machining (lathe, mill, waterjet), composite layups, and 3D printing.
  • Simulation & Software: Python, MATLAB, SolidWorks, AVL, XFOIL, and custom aerodynamic/thermal modeling tools.
